# COT-COT ESCAPE 🐔

A 3D co-op platformer in the browser: a band of hens climbs a 5 km chicken-coop tower all the way to the Moon.

The farm, the caves, the silo, the rooftop city, the storm, above the clouds, the auroras, the upper atmosphere and the Moon: **10 levels, 5 chapters**, a refuge every 250 m that saves your progress.

## ▶️ Play

- **[Play the alpha](https://t800lostinhumanity-svg.github.io/cot-cot-escape/)** — keyboard + mouse or gamepad
- **[See the first 3D art prototype](https://t800lostinhumanity-svg.github.io/cot-cot-escape/prototype-3d/)** — an orbit-camera showcase of the tower and the hens

## Gameplay

- **Jump, flap, glide** — your feathers are your stamina
- **Each kilometre teaches a new move**: peck-and-climb on wood, swing on an elastic worm from golden hooks, brood eggs so chicks follow you (and cushion big falls)
- **Choose your route** after each refuge: long and safe, short and technical, or team (2-hen lift)
- **Items**: rescue egg (places a nest), peacock feather (6 s of free flight), Boing mattress (inflatable trampoline)
- **Secrets**: 20 pages of Poulette's notebook, lost in 1987, hidden along the way
- **4 playable hens** — Cocotte, Paprika, Noisette and Pépite — each with goggly eyes, skinny legs and absurd accessories

## Co-op

Jump on a friend's head (pyramid), carry and throw her, lay your mattress for her, cluck at the same time to refill feathers, share chicks and nests.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Z Q S D / W A S D | Move (Shift: run) |
| Space | Jump · flap in the air · hold to glide |
| Mouse | Camera (click to capture) · wheel: zoom |
| 1 2 3 | Items |
| E | Carry / throw a friend · swing on hooks |
| C | Cluck! |
| R | Back to your highest nest |
| J | Poulette's notebook |
| Gamepad | Full support |

## Tech

- **Three.js** (WebGL): procedural textures, real-time shadows, custom sky shader, instanced clouds
- Custom **collision system** with spatial buckets, moving / fragile / bouncy platforms
- Pure **JavaScript / HTML / CSS**, no build step
- Built through **AI-assisted development** with Claude: concept, level design and art direction by me, code generated and iterated with the AI
- Made for the *Vibe Jam* (games built with AI)

## Online features

The original runs as a Claude artifact on claude.ai, where hens can play **together online**. On GitHub Pages the game runs in **solo mode** automatically.
